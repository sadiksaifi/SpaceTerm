# Unpair direct-network devices without blocking connection removal

Unpair Device applies to direct-network/WSS pairings. Account-local and SSH connections admit owning-account authority under [ADR 0027](0027-trust-paired-clients-equally.md); they have no SpaceTerm pairing to revoke. Removing a saved SSH connection ends its presentation and future ordinary reconnect intent without revoking SSH account access or deleting restricted pending originals under [ADR 0029](0029-pair-clients-automatically-during-ssh-bootstrap.md).

After confirmation, Unpair Device may target the initiating device, including the server's last Paired Device. Self-unpair immediately removes that connection's Workspace Attachments and saved connection and ends ordinary use, independently of server reachability. Unpairing another device leaves the initiating client's connection intact. An empty client view creates no replacement Workspace and kills no server-owned work.

The server durably records revocation, closes connections authorized by that device and releases its Pane control without stopping the server or Terminal Sessions. Revoked credentials cannot reconnect or resume control. Other devices and access to other servers remain unaffected.

Local removal and server-confirmed revocation have distinct outcomes. When confirmation is unavailable, a durable restricted background request survives client relaunch. A dismissible notice in the existing Servers section explains the unconfirmed result; hiding it does not cancel retries. Quiet continuation respects credential-provider and OS consent. Requested Connect/Resume obtains any needed interaction without a separate helper-preparation action.

Retries retain only the original route and pairing authority needed for that operation. They follow [ADR 0037](0037-resolve-shared-mutations-at-the-server.md) and cannot redirect through a changed SSH alias or newer pairing. Completion preserves newer or unrelated credentials, saved connections, attachments and shared keys. Retries never recreate removed presentation, acquire Pane control, Start Server, update either side or enroll a revoked device.

A revoked device may authenticate only to resolve its exact previously admitted revocation or confirm that its own exact pairing is already revoked. Confirmation of an already revoked self-pairing creates no new server effect or retained operation. This restricted authority handles a lost self-unpair acknowledgement or a concurrent owner's revocation, including the last Paired Device, without restoring ordinary access. The client retains a validated exact receipt before retiring its native credential reference. A failed or locked native store leaves cleanup pending; only explicit Resume or Retry may request credential-store interaction.

An interrupted or cancelled enrollment retains only its exact claim, pinned server, endpoint and native credential reference for restricted cancellation. It retains no invitation and performs no replacement enrollment. Once approval has won the race, cancellation revokes only the pairing created by that claim.
